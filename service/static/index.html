Potato check

Check a potato photo

Photograph one leaf or one tuber. You get the likely problem, how sure the model is, and where in the photo it looked.

Looking at the photo...

No potato to hand? Try one of the held-out test photos. The model has never seen them.

For a clear answer

  • Use daylight and keep the photo sharp.
  • Fill the frame with one leaf or one tuber.
  • Keep the affected area in view, not just the healthy part.

A screening aid, not a diagnosis. Confirm with an extension officer before treating a crop or discarding seed.
